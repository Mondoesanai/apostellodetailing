How Often Should You Detail Your Car?

Published September 24, 2026

A good rule of thumb is a full detail two to four times a year, with a quick maintenance wash in between. But the right schedule really depends on how you use your vehicle and where it spends its time.

Why "it depends" is the honest answer

A car that sits in a garage and mostly runs errands around town holds up very differently than a work truck parked outside, a daily commuter, or a vehicle that hauls kids, pets, and gear. Dust, tree sap, pollen, road salt, and interior spills all add up at different rates, so the right interval is really about your driving and parking habits, not a fixed number.

Signs it's time for a detail

Instead of watching the calendar, watch the vehicle. It's usually time when you notice:

  • Water no longer beads on the paint, or the finish looks dull
  • Interior surfaces feel sticky, dusty, or have lingering odors
  • Carpets and seats show visible stains or ground-in dirt
  • It's been several months since the last thorough clean

A simple starting schedule

If you're not sure where to start, this general approach works for most drivers in our area:

  • Every 2-4 weeks: A quick exterior wash to keep dirt, pollen, and road grime from building up.
  • Every 3-4 months: A more thorough detail covering the interior, wheels, and paint.
  • As needed: After a road trip, a muddy weekend, or before selling the vehicle.

Vehicles that see heavy daily use, kids, pets, or lots of outdoor parking may do better on the shorter end of that range.

Making it easier on yourself
