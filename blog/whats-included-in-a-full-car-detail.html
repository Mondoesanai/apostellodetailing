What's Included in a Full Car Detail?

October 20, 2026

A full car detail is a deep, top-to-bottom clean that goes well beyond a standard car wash. Instead of a quick rinse and vacuum, a detail addresses the interior, exterior, wheels, and glass in detail — covering the spots a regular wash usually skips.

Exterior work

On the outside, a full detail typically includes a hand wash, wheel and tire cleaning, and attention to areas like door jambs, mirrors, and badges. Exterior glass is cleaned, and depending on the condition of the paint, additional steps like clay bar treatment or wax/sealant protection may be part of the process. The goal is a clean, protected surface — not just a shiny one for a day or two.

Interior work

Inside the vehicle, a full detail usually covers a thorough vacuum of seats, carpets, and trunk; wiping down the dash, console, and door panels; cleaning interior glass; and addressing cup holders, vents, and other small spaces that collect dust and debris. Depending on your vehicle's materials, seats and carpets can be shampooed or spot-cleaned, and leather can be conditioned.

What can vary between vehicles

Not every vehicle needs the same level of attention. A daily driver with light dust is a different job than an SUV that's hauled mulch, kids, and gear all summer — or a boat that's spent the season on the water. Because of this, the exact scope of a full detail can be adjusted based on your vehicle's condition and what matters most to you. If you're not sure what your vehicle needs, it's worth asking before booking.

Mobile detailing makes it easy

Because Apostello Detailing comes to you, a full detail can be done right in your driveway or at your workplace, whether it's a car, SUV, or boat.
