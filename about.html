The Story Behind Apostello

BUILT FROM SCRATCH.
ONE CAR AT A TIME.

Shiloh started Apostello Detailing the way most real businesses start — with more ambition than equipment, and a willingness to show up early. What began as detailing cars for friends and family turned into a mobile operation that now serves Franklin County and the surrounding area, one driveway, marina, and parking lot at a time.

The name "Apostello" comes from the Greek word meaning "sent out" — which is exactly how this business runs. No shop, no waiting room, no dropping your car off and hoping it's ready by end of day. Shiloh comes to you, with everything needed to turn a tired interior or a chalky hull into something that turns heads again.

As a young entrepreneur, Shiloh built this business on a simple bet: that people would rather pay for real quality and real convenience than deal with a big-box car wash that treats every vehicle the same. Every package, every add-on, and every recurring plan on this site was priced to make that bet worth it for both of you.

This is still day one energy, applied every single appointment. Book a detail and see it for yourself.
